Interactive map. Everything shown on the map is also described in the story text beside it. With the map focused, use the arrow keys to pan and the plus and minus keys to zoom. Where markers are shown, Tab to a marker and press Enter or Space to open its text, and Escape to close it. Where a comparison slider is shown, Tab to the slider and use the left and right arrow keys to move it.
